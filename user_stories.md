# User Stories for Smart Clinic

## Admin Stories
* **As an Admin**, I want to add new doctors to the system, **so that** they are available for clients to book appointments with.
* **As an Admin**, I want to remove or deactivate doctors who no longer work at the clinic, **so that** patients cannot book appointments with unavailable staff.
* **As an Admin**, I want to manage working hours, holidays, and weekends, **so that** clients know when the clinic is open.

## Patient Stories
* **As a Patient**, I want to book an appointment, **so that** I can select a convenient doctor, date, and time.
* **As a Patient**, I want to view my prescriptions, **so that** I can easily access dosage instructions and purchase required medications.
* **As a Patient**, I want to see my appointment history, **so that** I can keep track of past doctor visits and medical advice.

## Doctor Stories
* **As a Doctor**, I want to view my daily schedule and patient list, **so that** I can prepare for upcoming visits in advance.
* **As a Doctor**, I want to issue prescriptions digitally, **so that** patients receive them instantly without unnecessary paperwork.
* **As a Doctor**, I want to access a patient's prescription history and medical documents, **so that** I can make informed treatment decisions.
